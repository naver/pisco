## Lessons (what I would tell the next campaign)
- **Four cheap checks found the target that months of training arms missed.** One inference probe (the page's
  words as text slots beside its image slots, +5.0 pt [+2.5, +7.6], n = 1,000 paired questions) plus one 100-page
  overfit (word recall .921 vs .702, n = 100 pages) pointed at what stage 2 teaches, not at slot count, padding or
  data caps. About 21 GPU-hours. Probe before you train.
- **Write the repeat's new items into the law before the first read.** The two document sets the law named for a
  repeat reused the gating questions with other pages; the wake had to tighten the law to "the next 500 disjoint
  questions" mid-campaign. Name items by `query_id` range up front.
- **A chained child must not die with a parent whose files are complete.** A requeued training attempt hit its own
  overwrite guard (exit 3) and its recall child never ran; two builds failed their own health rule on complete
  sets. Fixed in the launchers (`pt_overfit.sbatch` 875feaab, `build_text_sets.py` 6b438662); the next campaign's
  launchers should exit 0 when the output is already complete.
- **Cancelled never-run children ate the cell cap.** Five of them used the cells a second overfit seed needed, so
  that repeat became an owner question. Count only cells that ran, or leave slack for re-chaining.
- **Shared code moved under a running arm.** `pisco/model.py` changed between the two reads of check 3 (another
  chat's one-row pad guard). Re-running the baseline cell on the new code (500 / 500 identical answers) is what made
  the two reads poolable; keep that habit.
- **83% of the window was the owner's answer, not idle work.** After the four verdicts the loop could only wait
  (and a weekly usage hold took 3 days). End a probe campaign with one question and stand down when it is answered.
